SDS Theory Group

Rigorous algorithmic and mathematical foundations fordata, learning, optimization, and decision systems.

We are a focused theory community at the School of Data Science, CUHK-Shenzhen. Our research connects algorithms, optimization, learning, economics, and discrete mathematics with the foundational questions behind modern data-driven systems.
