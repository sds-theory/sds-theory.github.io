Consistent Hashing and Its Applications

SDS Topical SeminarUpcoming

Consistent Hashing and Its Applications

Research visit: Nov 9–10, 2026Mon, Nov 9, 2026, 11:00 AM-12:00 PM (Beijing time)Prof. Shaofeng Jiang, Center on Frontiers of Computing Studies, Peking UniversityRoom 401, Dao Yuan BuildingHost: Prof. Aditi Dudeja

Consistent hashing partitions a metric space into bounded-diameter regions independently of the data, so that every compact subset intersects only a small number of regions. This talk introduces consistent hashing, its efficient construction, and applications to high-dimensional algorithm design, especially clustering, and concludes with open questions. The seminar is delivered in English. Prof. Shaofeng Jiang visits on November 9-10, 2026.
