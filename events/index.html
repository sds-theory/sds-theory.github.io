Faster Dynamic Graph Clustering with Hierarchical Graph Contraction

SDS Topical SeminarUpcoming

Faster Dynamic Graph Clustering with Hierarchical Graph Contraction

Research visit: Oct 15–16, 2026Thu, Oct 15, 2026, 11:00 AM-12:00 PM (Beijing time)Prof. He Sun, Center for Algorithms and Learning Theory, SIAT, Chinese Academy of SciencesRoom 413, Zhi Xin BuildingHost: Prof. Aditi Dudeja

This talk studies clustering in evolving graphs as new edges and vertices arrive. A hierarchical data structure maintains the changing cluster structure with faster amortised update and query times, with experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets. The talk is based on joint work with Suranjan De and Steinar Laenen appearing at NeurIPS 2026. The seminar will be delivered in English. He Sun will visit on October 15-16, 2026.
